Welcome!

I am a 2nd year PhD student at the National Technical University of Athens and a research fellow at Archimedes AI, advised by Nikos Komodakis. I recently interned at Runway AI with Jon Granskog. Not so long ago, I obtained my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the same university.

My research sits at the intersection of Generative Modeling and Representation Learning. These days, I mostly play around with diffusion models — both image and language.
